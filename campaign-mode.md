# Campaign Mode

Campaign Mode is the story journey through the **Nexus Chronicles**. Chapters appear as a shelf of graphic-novel volumes; reached chapters can be previewed and launched, while later volumes unlock as you make progress.

## Choose a route

Each chapter is a forward-moving map with one starting point and a boss at its end. Branches let you choose between routes and optional encounters before paths reconnect. A run can take you through:

- Card Battles and tougher Elite Card Battles
- Card Vendors
- Scripted story events
- Treasure pulls
- Brawler encounters
- A chapter Boss Battle

Your route choices determine which encounters and opportunities you meet on the way to the boss. Read node previews before committing when they are available; a vendor visit or optional fight can change what resources you bring to the next match.

## Progress and rewards

Campaign progress is saved independently for each chapter. Completing a chapter records the clear and unlocks the next volume. Battles and exploration can award Elemental Shards, while treasure and vendor nodes offer additional collection opportunities. Exact rewards depend on the node and current chapter content.

Campaign matches use your Hero and a legal deck. Some story encounters may add authored dialogue, special rules, or unique presentation without changing the underlying match engine. A Brawler node is a side-scrolling detour; winning completes the node, while cancelling leaves it available to retry.

For the card-battle basics, see [First Match](getting-started.md). For the campaign's side-scrolling encounters and other modes, see [Side Modes & Mini-Games](side-modes.md).
