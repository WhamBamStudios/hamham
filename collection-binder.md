# Collection Binder

The Collection Binder is your card library: a place to browse what you own and inspect individual copies. Owned cards and Heroes appear as collection entries, with quantities and collectible treatments tracked separately.

## Browsing your collection

Turn through binder pages to see your cards. Each page holds up to twelve cards. Select or inspect a card to bring up its full face, including rules text and details that may be abbreviated in a grid or deck list. Collector variants remain distinct from the standard treatment, even when two copies share the same card definition.

The Binder is an inventory view, not a deck. Owning a card does not add it to a saved deck automatically, and a binder copy cannot be included in more decks than the format allows. Use the [Deck Builder](deck-builder.md) to assemble a legal list from cards you own.

## Managing copies

The displayed quantity is the number of copies available in your collection. Copies may be reserved by a feature such as a listing or otherwise unavailable in a particular mode. Before you shard or offer a copy, check the remaining quantity and its collectible treatment.

For turning extra copies into collection currency, see [Sharding & Crafting](sharding-and-crafting.md). For card trades and auctions, see [Trading & Auctions](trading-and-auctions.md).
