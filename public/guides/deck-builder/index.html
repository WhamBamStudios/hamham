Deck Builder

The Deck Builder turns your collection into a battle-ready list around a chosen Hero. It checks the saved deck against your owned copies and the selected Hero’s affinities before allowing you to save.

Building a deck

  1. Choose a Hero and give the deck a name.
  2. Browse or filter cards by type, rarity, and Element.
  3. Add cards you own and adjust quantities until the deck is legal.
  4. Review the deck count and type breakdown, then confirm Save.

The current campaign deck builder supports up to five named Hero decks and a primary deck selection. Its deck-size range is 10–60 cards. The builder validates card ownership, Hero affinity, and an eight-copy ceiling. A multi-affinity Hero can use cards matching any of its affinities.

A few practical tips

  • Build a curve you can actually pay for: a powerful card is less useful when its Aeon cost is out of reach.
  • Include cards that support your Hero’s Element and plan, but do not assume your whole list must share one Element.
  • Check the full card text, not just its type or rarity.
  • Revisit your deck after gaining cards, adjusting a Hero’s rank, or switching to a mode with different rules.

Deck legality is mode-specific. The game validates the list you submit, so follow the current builder’s warnings if a particular encounter or format has extra restrictions. For card costs and affinities, see Elemental Aeon and Hero Cards.
