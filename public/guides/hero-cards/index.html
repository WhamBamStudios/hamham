Hero Cards: Affinity, Resolve & Limit Break

Your Hero is more than a portrait: it defines your starting leader, elemental plan, and signature abilities. A starter Hero typically begins with 30 Life and one affinity, though authored Heroes can differ or have multiple affinities.

Affinity and Hero Power

During Aeon Generation, a Hero produces its effective amount of Aeon for each of its affinities. A Fire/Light Hero that produces one Aeon generates one Fire and one Light. Your Hero Power is a separate action with its own cost and is normally usable once during your Main phase.

Two kinds of Limit Break

Limit Break rank is collection progression outside a match. The standard rank path is None → R → SR → SSR → UR. Rank-ups cost shards matching the Hero’s primary affinity; the default costs are:

Rank-upElemental Shard cost
None → R25
R → SR50
SR → SSR80
SSR → UR100

A rank can improve Attack, maximum Life, Aeon production, abilities, Hero Power, or presentation. The exact upgrade depends on the Hero’s authored progression.

Resolve is an in-match meter. Each Hero with a Resolve kit has its own rules for gaining Resolve from match events, plus a cap (six is the default). Resolve may unlock threshold effects, fuel Resolve Techniques, or build toward that Hero’s dramatic full-meter Limit Break. Techniques can have rank requirements, costs, and specific timing windows.

These systems are related but not interchangeable: ranking a Hero uses collection shards; gaining Resolve happens during a match. Reaching UR does not automatically fill the Resolve meter, and having Resolve does not spend your collection shards.

Hero-specific kits

Resolve triggers, techniques, thresholds, and the full-meter Limit Break are authored per Hero. Some effects become stronger at higher ranks, and UR may enhance the final Limit Break. Inspect the selected Hero’s full rules text to see exactly how it earns Resolve and when each ability can be used.
