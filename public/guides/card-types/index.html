Card Types

Every card has a primary Element and a type that tells you how it generally enters or affects the match. A Hero is selected as your leader; the other card types make up your battle deck.

Unit

Units are your fighters on the battlefield. They occupy positions, attack or block, and may have static, triggered, or activated abilities. A Unit’s Attack and Life, along with its lane, position, movement, and card text, shape how it fights.

Spell

Spells create effects when played: damage, movement, protection, card draw, and many stranger tricks. A Spell’s targets, timing, and exact outcome come from its rules text. Some can be answered during an interrupt window.

Artifact

Artifacts are persistent objects that can provide passive effects, triggers, or abilities you activate by paying their cost. They are not Units and do not occupy a Unit position.

Secret

Secrets hide a condition from the opponent and trigger when the required event occurs. Their timing and effect are defined by the card; not every unexpected effect is a Secret, despite what your opponent will claim afterward.

Domain

Domains are persistent elemental resource cards. A Domain has no ordinary casting cost or Unit-style Attack and Life. It can generate Aeon or provide ongoing effects, and may affect one or more lanes. In the current turn flow, a player may normally establish one Domain during the Environment phase.

Hero

Your Hero is your leader and the center of your strategy. A Hero has Life, Attack, elemental affinity, energy production, and a Hero Power; some also have Resolve techniques and a Limit Break. Hero cards are collected and progressed separately from ordinary deck cards. See Hero Cards.

Primary Element is not the same as cost

A card’s primary Element describes its identity and is used for things such as affinity, card framing, and shard rewards. Its printed Aeon cost is separate: a card may carry an Element identity but ask for a different combination of elemental, paired, or generic Aeon to play.

See Elemental Aeon and Card Stats for more.
