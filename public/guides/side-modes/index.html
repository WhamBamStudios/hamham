Side Modes & Mini-Games

Not every scrap is fought over a card table. WHAM! BAM! HAM-HAM! includes several alternate encounters and experimental modes; access can vary by build.

Rift Survival

Rift Survival is a top-down survival encounter that can appear on a campaign route. Move around an arena while your Hero automatically fires toward the pointer. Corrupted enemies arrive in waves; defeating them earns experience and opens power-up choices. The global rift timer eventually summons a mini-boss. Defeat it to win before your Hero’s Life runs out.

Power-ups can improve attack damage, fire rate, movement, Life, projectile speed, size, count, or piercing, and can provide immediate healing. Which choices appear depends on the encounter.

Fatal Flurry

Fatal Flurry is a separate, side-view, turn-based party mode. Build a team of Heroes and a Skill deck. During your team’s turn, each living Hero takes one action: use a legal Skill with that Hero’s own energy, or pass. When all living Heroes have acted, the opposing turn begins.

Guard absorbs damage until that Hero’s next turn. Defeat the other team, or have more total Hero health when the match clock expires; a tied total is a draw.

Brawler encounters

Campaign Brawler nodes fling your Hero into a side-scrolling beat-’em-up universe. Clear the encounter to complete the node and return to the campaign map. These are a change of pace from lane-based card combat, not a second deck-battle ruleset.

Action RPG arena — prototype

The standalone 3D Action RPG arena is an explicitly experimental mode. It features light and heavy melee attacks, ranged spells, pursuing enemies, and a simple win/defeat loop. It is a gameplay prototype rather than a full campaign or collection progression path.

For the main campaign journey, see Campaign Mode.
